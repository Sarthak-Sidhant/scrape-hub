What the throttle does

The runners never decide how hard to hit a portal; the hub does, once a minute, for each portal separately.

Searches open ("windows")How many search sessions our runners may keep open on the portal at once. This is the load that knocks portals over, so it is the main dial.
LimitThe most searches the throttle will ever allow on that portal. It starts at 32 (6 for the small PSU portals) and moves up to 40 or down as the portal shows what it can take.
Every minuteAnswers under 2.1 s: allow more. 3-6 s: allow a little less. Over 6 s or many dropped connections: halve. Portal not answering: hold everything, then restart from 1.
LearningFalls over: limit cut to 70% of where it fell. Chokes near the limit: cut to 80%. Fast at its limit for 10 minutes: +2 (+4 when answering in under a second). It never climbs back to a level where it fell before.
