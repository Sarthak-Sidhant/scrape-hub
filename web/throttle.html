How the throttle drove every portal

Every minute the hub looks at how each portal answered our runners and sets two dials: windows (searches in flight, the load that knocks a portal over) and threads per process. It climbs while answers are fast, eases off when they slow, halves when they are bad, holds everything when the portal stops answering, and learns a lower ceiling where a portal choked or fell. This page draws every one of those decisions.

The fleet at a glance

One row per portal, one column per time slice. The brighter the cell, the more windows the throttle allowed; red is the portal not answering; a mark on a cell is a decision worth knowing about. Hover for the details.
